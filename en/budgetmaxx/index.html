Privacy: works without internet

No account. No cloud. No bank connection.

BudgetMaxx is a single HTML file that runs in your browser, even without internet. Your data is stored locally on your computer and can optionally be encrypted. This page doesn't load anything from outside either.

Your money stays home. So does Max.

Note: because everything is stored locally, you make your own backups. The app helps you with that.

Honest about AI

BudgetMaxx is built by Luna, with the help of AI agents. We don't hide it.

The app itself uses no AI and no internet. Your data goes nowhere.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, and no server your data goes to.

What does it cost?

Nothing. BudgetMaxx is free. If you'd like to give something back you can donate, but you don't have to.

Is it open source?

Yes. The code is public, so anyone can see what the app does.

Does the app use AI?

No. The app was built with the help of AI agents, but the app itself uses no AI and never connects to the internet.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your own computer, in your browser. It goes nowhere. You can optionally encrypt it.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. It's a single HTML file that works offline.

What if I lose my computer?

Make regular backups. Without a backup your data can't be recovered, because we don't have it.

Is there an English version?

Yes. You can switch the whole app to English in Settings, including the PDF.

Is this financial advice?

No. BudgetMaxx is a calculation aid to help you get an overview.
