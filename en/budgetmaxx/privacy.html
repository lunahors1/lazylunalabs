Privacy

Privacy statement

Please note: this text has not yet been checked by a lawyer and is not legal advice. The beta form is not yet connected; once it goes live, what is written below applies.

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

In short

  • The app collects nothing. No account, no tracking, no connection to a server. Everything stays on your own device.
  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics.
  • The website host, like any host, briefly keeps technical server logs (including your IP address).
  • Only if you sign up as a beta tester or email us do we process your first name and email address (and whatever you write yourself).

Who is responsible?

Lazy Luna Labs, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 89453980. Contact: hello@lazylunalabs.com.

The app

BudgetMaxx runs in your browser and stores everything on your own device. We receive no amounts, no settings and no usage data, because the app sends nothing away. Because we do not have your data, we cannot restore it either: please make your own backup (see Prove for yourself that it works offline, in Dutch). The app is open-source under GPL-3.0.

The website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and loads no fonts, scripts or other components from other parties.

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., United States). When you request a page, GitHub processes technical data, such as your IP address, the time and the page requested, in server logs. We do not see or use this data.
  • Why and legal basis: to be able to show the site and keep it secure; our legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Retention: determined by GitHub. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Domain: the domain name and DNS are managed at Squarespace (US).

Beta sign-up

If you sign up as a beta tester, we only ask for your first name and email address.

  • Purpose: to invite you to the beta and to email you about it. The beta is closed: the download link is only emailed to the testers Luna chooses.
  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)), given by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Retention: at most 3 months after launch. After that we delete your data. Only if you separately agree (optional tick box) do we keep your email address longer, to invite you to test new apps from Lazy Luna Labs.
  • Processor: the form runs through a form tool. We use Google Forms (Google, United States) for this.
  • Sharing: we do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else, other than the service providers named here.
  • We email using BCC, so nobody sees anyone else's address.

Feedback questionnaires are anonymous and do not ask for amounts or screenshots with real figures.

Email

If you email hello@lazylunalabs.com, we process your email address and message in order to reply (legitimate interest, or to carry out your request). The address forwards to a private mailbox of Luna's. The forwarding runs through Mailgun (United States), which temporarily processes your message to deliver it. We do not keep emails longer than needed to deal with your question.

Transfers outside the EU

GitHub, Squarespace, Mailgun and Google are based in the United States. Your data may therefore be processed outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable, these parties participate in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses as a safeguard.

Your rights

You may view, correct or delete your data, have processing restricted, object to it, ask for your data to be transferred, and withdraw your consent at any time. Email us; we will respond within one month. If you cannot resolve the matter with us, you can lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
