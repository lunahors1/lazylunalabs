Quick-start guide

The beta is open. Free, open source and offline. No account, no cloud.

  1. Download and open

    Download the BudgetMaxx file (a single HTML file) and double-click it. It opens in your browser and also works without internet. Chrome and Safari are tested. Keep the file somewhere permanent so you always open it the same way.

  2. Fill in the foundation

    Follow the short wizard: income, fixed costs, savings pots and loans. You only have to do it once. Not sure about an amount? Enter an estimate, you can change it later.

  3. Tick off this month

    The monthly overview follows your pay period. Tick off what has come in and what has been paid, and you see what is still open.

  4. Budget coach

    The Budget coach works out a daily budget from your pot, up to your next pay day. That way you see what you can still spend today.

  5. Your day

    A pop-up showing what comes in today and what goes out. You can switch it on or off and choose the time in Settings.

  6. Make and restore a backup

    Your data lives only on your own device; we have no copy. So make a backup regularly (the app helps you) and keep the file somewhere you know, for example a USB stick. You restore with that backup file in the app. Try this once so you know it works.

  7. If something goes wrong

    Close the tab and open the file again. Still not working? Email hello@lazylunalabs.com and describe what happened. Never send screenshots with real amounts; use made-up figures instead.

This is a beta: errors are possible, so keep your own records alongside the app for the first few weeks. BudgetMaxx is a calculation aid, not financial advice. Questionnaires: after week 1 and week 3 (anonymous, no amounts).
