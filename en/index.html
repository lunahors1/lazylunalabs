About

Hi, I'm Luna 👋

After university I finally started earning a bit more, yet at the end of every month there was nothing left. After countless spreadsheets I helped a friend get on top of their finances, and realised how much I enjoyed it. That sparked the idea: build tools that give others that same calm.

I had never written code before. AI and agents suddenly made building possible. Not with one simple prompt, but with a lot of testing. The first result is Budgetmaxx, and I'm proud of it.
